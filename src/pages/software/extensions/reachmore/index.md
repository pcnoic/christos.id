A browser extension to help automate and manage your LinkedIn connection requests safely and efficiently.

## Features

- Automated connection requests with configurable delays
- Daily connection limits to stay within LinkedIn's guidelines
- Simple and intuitive interface
- Completely client-side - your data stays on your device
