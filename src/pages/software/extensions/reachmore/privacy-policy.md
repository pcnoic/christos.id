**Last Updated:** January 12, 2025

ReachMore ("the Extension") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your data.

## 1. Data Collection and Usage

The Extension **does not collect, transmit, or sell** any of your personal data to us or any third parties.

- **Local Storage**: All data related to your LinkedIn network expansion progress (e.g., connection counts, daily quotas, settings) is stored locally on your device using your browser's local storage capabilities (`chrome.storage.local`).
- **No Remote Servers**: The Extension operates entirely within your browser. It does not communicate with any external servers owned or operated by the developers of ReachMore.

## 2. Permissions

The Extension requires specific permissions to function:

- **`tabs` & `activeTab`**: Used to identify the LinkedIn tab to automate connection requests.
- **`storage`**: Used to save your settings and progress locally.
- **`alarms`**: Used to schedule background checks for safe, throttled automation.
- **`scripting`**: Used to interact with the LinkedIn webpage to perform the "Connect" action on your behalf.

## 3. LinkedIn Data

The Extension interacts with the LinkedIn website to automate clicks. It does not harvest, scrape, or store your LinkedIn profile data, login credentials, or the profiles of other users, other than temporarily counting the actions it performs.

## 4. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted in this file.
